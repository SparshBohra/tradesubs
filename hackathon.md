# 🏛️ Karma Street

## 🚀 Reddit Stock Exchange Simulator

Karma Street transforms Reddit into a dynamic, gamified stock market where subreddits are traded like stocks. Buy, sell, and track community engagement in real-time!

### 📈 Elevator Pitch

What if Reddit was a stock market? Karma Street lets you trade subreddits based on their engagement, using virtual karma dollars. Bet on trending communities, track sector indexes, and become the ultimate Reddit investor!

## 🧠 Concept

Karma Street simulates a real-time trading experience where:
- Every subreddit functions as a tradable "stock"
- Prices are determined by Reddit engagement metrics
- Trading happens with virtual Karma Dollars

## 💡 Key Features

### 🔍 Sectoral Indexes
- Tech Index
- Crypto Index
- Meme Index
- Finance Index
- Super Index (overall market average)

### 📊 Stock Price Calculation
Subreddit prices are dynamically calculated using a sophisticated formula that considers:
- Post volume
- Comment activity
- Karma growth
- Engagement ratios
- Volatility
- Momentum
- Speculation
- Decay factors

## 🎨 UI Highlights
- Clean trading interface

## ⚠️ Current Limitations
- Limited to ~30 tracked subreddits
- Potential issues with private subreddits
- Dependent on Reddit API access
